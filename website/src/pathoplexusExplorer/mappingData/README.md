# Pathoplexus Explorer mapping data

This directory contains lookup tables. The functions that use them are in `../domain/`.

- `subregions.ts` uses the UN M49 geographic classification. `countryNames.ts` maps country names to those used by the reports and maps.
- Most maps in `website/public/mapData/` use Natural Earth. Kenya uses geoBoundaries. The Argentina map and the internal boundaries separating Moscow from Moscow Oblast and Altai Republic from Altai Krai use [Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1, version 5.1.1](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/). Argentina retains all 24 features, including Ciudad de Buenos Aires (AR-C) and Provincia de Buenos Aires (AR-B). Qualified localities use Argentina’s [Georef directory](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/georef/descarga-de-la-base-completa) and provincial records for [Ingeniero Bunge](https://boletinoficial.gba.gob.ar/secciones/12083/ver), the [Las Tunas area](https://www.ambiente.gba.gob.ar/ParticipacionCiudadana/EIAS%20Arroyo%20Las%20Tunas%20-DPH.pdf), and [Laferrere](https://sistemas.transporte.gba.gob.ar/Transporte/puntosRegistro.php). The bare name “Buenos Aires” can refer to either area.
- The Laos map uses the [Lao National Geographic Department boundaries published by UN OCHA, COD-AB v01](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-lao), under [CC BY 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/). The geometry is simplified and display names follow the report. Vientiane Capital, Vientiane Province, and Xaisomboon have separate features.
- Bangladesh, Central African Republic, Guinea, Indonesia, Madagascar, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Sierra Leone, and Viet Nam use [UN OCHA Common Operational Datasets](https://data.humdata.org/organization/ocha-fiss) under [CC BY 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/). Each map records the country dataset and version. Indonesia’s district polygons follow the [June 2026 BIG province roster](https://github.com/dmxsan/indonesia-admin-boundaries/blob/3a805d141ef0a7ad2b5a0c2b78abe108bf04f623/metadata/catalog.json). Myanmar’s Bago and Shan subdivisions are grouped by region and state.
- Uganda uses the [uganda-locale district and city polygons](https://github.com/kakandemanwell/uganda/tree/bb3ee6b93953460db4fa39f1508031f54d84dcbb/dist/geo), derived from Uganda Bureau of Statistics census boundaries, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The ten separate cities are cut out of their surrounding districts. A name shared by a district and city requires a qualifier.
- The Philippines uses [James Faeldon’s Philippine Statistics Authority maps and province hierarchy, PSGC 2026 Q3](https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-json-maps/tree/680e32e62baeccbcd8845d3b1387bc67c1baf78a). South Korea uses [October 2026 administrative boundaries](https://github.com/vuski/admdongkor/tree/078ef4ac13b8bfeb215d2eabace49f4fdea71d5e). This data is derived from administrative-dong boundaries released by Statistics Korea SGIS (https://sgis.kostat.go.kr) under KOGL Type 1, modified by vuski/admdongkor (https://github.com/vuski/admdongkor), distributed under CC BY 4.0. The maps are simplified for display.
- `divisionNames.json` maps division names and codes to areas on each map. `divisionPlaces.json` maps places such as cities to the divisions that contain them. Both dictionaries are keyed by country and then by the exact area name on the map. Each area lists its alternative names or places. The area name itself is recognized automatically. A recorded division title such as “Provincia di” in Italy or “Département de” in France can qualify a listed name. See the [division coverage script](../../../scripts/admin1Names/README.md) for how to check these tables.
- `hostTaxa.json` is [generated from NCBI taxonomy](../../../scripts/hostTaxa/README.md).
- `missingValues.ts` contains placeholder values, including INSDC terms for missing geographic data.

See [data interpretation](../../../../docs/pathoplexus-explorer-domain-decisions.md) for how the reports and matrix use the mappings.
